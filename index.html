A little more context

I'm Arijit, a frontend developer with a background in design, which shows up in how I build: type scales, spacing, and motion get the same attention as component architecture.

Most of my work lives in React and TypeScript, shipping product UI, internal tools, and the occasional design system. I care about interfaces that stay fast and legible as they grow — not just on day one.

Outside of client work, I contribute to a couple of open-source component libraries and write short notes on frontend architecture and accessibility.
